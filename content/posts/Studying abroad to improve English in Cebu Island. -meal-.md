The thing that I was looking forward to the most is good meal in Cebu.I heard that there are many kind of good meals and restaurants and I expected it.And the meal which is provided in language school acquires well-reputation.I was looking forward it as well.

However I felt something unusual in my body when it spent 2 days from arrival in Cebu.After eating something, I had stomach ache.

I thought about causes of it and found the following 3 points.

### Difference in water hardness
Water in Cebu is hard water whereas Japanese one is soft water.

This is why most of Japanese have an upset stomach for a week from arrival.

Because there were water servers at everywhere in the language school, there were no opportunity to drink tap water. Even when brushing of teeth or gargling I always used water server. but I could not avoid stomach ache.

### Stress due to change of lifestyle
I may have had some stresses due to living with foreigners. Our room had 2 Japanese and 2 Taiwanese.it reminded me of the dormitory life when I was college student.

### Overcooling by air conditioning
The climate in Cebu is everlasting summer. On the other hand, in a room It was very cool.(ex. Classroom, movie theater, bus) It seems that it had acted as a trigger to make me feel stomach ache.

I had felt stomach ache until I came back to Japan.If I ate too much, I felt a pain.I always focused on how to get a minimum of nutrition during meal at school. Thus I cannot remember the taste of the meal at school :‑o.

However I went to some places to seek delicious meals while seeing my body condition.

In this article I will introduce 3 theme which was particularly impressive to me.

- Steak at ACACIA STEAKHOUSE
- Fast Food in (Chowking/Jollibee)
- Turks Pita Sandwich

## Steak at ACACIA STEAKHOUSE

When I requested a taxi driver to take to ACACIA STEAKHOUSE, it seemed ambiguous place for him. Thus I instructed the way to the restaurant by using Google Map. As you can see the map above, this restaurant is located along the narrow lane.

![](/images/StakeHouse1-1024x688.jpg)

Flat Iron Steak 495 peso（about 1200 yen）

I requested medium steak.I could feel elastic of steak every time when I bite it. It was filling food for me.

## Fast Food in (Chowking/Jollibee)
My gourmet friend introduced his recommended fast food restaurants to me.If There was time, I thought I would love to stop by these places.

Chowking and Jollibee are fast food restaurant.Chowking serves Chinese cuisine and Jollibee serves hamburger and pasta.Although I was anxious about whether I can find these restaurants, both of them were seen frequently in city.

In Philippines Jollibee is more popular than McDonalds and has No.1 fast food market share.

The time when Jollibee open a store in Japan might come someday.

### Chowking
![](/images/Chowking-991x1024.jpg)

In order to have lunch easily, I ordered the menu above.It was simple-tasting and good.

### Jollibee
![](/images/Jollibee-1024x749.jpg)

At this time My condition was fine and I ordered 5 food including drink.There were 3 carbohydrates, a fried chicken and a cup of cola.Of course after that, I gained weight.

Filipino like sweet taste. A ketchup belonging fried chicken is based banana instead of tomato.Regarding banana ketchup, I think opnions seems to be divided.

All foods were so good except for banana ketchup.

## Turks Pita Sandwich
The most impressive food in Cebu was the Turks Pita Sandwich.

![](/images/Turks-e1578566671928.png)

I went to Cebu from Narita airport via Manila.  
During staying Manila, I purchased it for my first time.

When I had a bite, I was be momentarily speechless.  
This was because I had never experienced the texture and flavor of it.  
To say the least, it was very delicious.

The price was affordable (about 140 yen) and the quantity was just right.  
I ate it every time I went out. It seems to be popular in Philippine and it could be found anywhere.

When I came back to Japan and talked about it with my co-worker from Philippine, I understood it is Turkish food as "Turks" suggests.Although I investigated whether there is in Japan, I could not the information.I think it is likely to be middle east food "Kebab".I have never eaten it.So I would like to try it someday.

I deeply hope Turks Pita Sandwich will come to Japan.

When you visit Cebu, please try it!
