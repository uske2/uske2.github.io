I had only one opportunity to enjoy weekend during stay in Cebu. In this article I will introduce the following activities which I enjoyed from 30Nov2019 (Sat) to 01Dec2019 (Sun).

- Whale Shark Watching 30Nov2019 (Sat)
- Tumalog Water Falls 30Nov2019 (Sat)
- Climing Osmena Peak 01Dec2019 (Sun)

### Whale Shark Watching
I talked about how to spend weekend in Cebu with 3 Japanese who enrolled in the language school at the same time. And we decided to go to watch Whale Sharks.

In the early morning, we got together at 5:00 and headed toward South Bus Terminal.

Whale Sharks come to near the coast in order to eat bait in the morning. When they finish their meal ,return back to sea around 11:00. Also it takes 4 hours to go to the destination called Oslob by bus. When I think back, we should have departed more early.

Although it was early morning, South Bus Terminal was very crowded with local people and visitors. When some enigmatic people in bus terminal said something to me, I was careful to them immediately. But once I told that I want to go to Oslob, they told kindly me the gate where the bus heading to Oslob depart from.

In the bus it was very cool. I recommend you bring something to put on.  
When approaching the destination, bus staff informed us of arrival shouting “Whale Shark !”.

Once we arrived, we completed the reception for watching Whale Shark at a shop near the bus stop. There were following 3 options. We decided to scuba because scuba diving allows us to watch Whale Shark closely. The price was 1800 peso (about 4600 yen) which was highest price in the 3 options.

- Watching from a boat
- Snorkeling
- Scuba diving

I waited for 30 minutes. And then after instructors finished guiding previous visitors, they came back to waiting place. They encouraged us to get ready for scuba diving as soon as they came back. This was because that Whale Sharks was about to be back to sea shortly.

After I wore a wet suit, carried an air bottle on my back and wrapped a weight about 5kg around hip, I felt nervous suddenly.  
We took a simple class about how to breath and some meanings of signals which were exchanged between an instructor and a visitor. The instructor taught us repeatedly the importance not to become panicky in water. He told us about some situations when it is common to become panicky and solutions to handle these situations. The following main contents are as below.

---

**Water enters into goggle due to nose breathing.**

⇒ If you turn up in the sea, water would transfer to at the top of the goggle. Your sight will be clear.

**Breathing becomes shallow due to shortage of oxygen.**

⇒ when breathing you had better to breathe deeply. (But flotation power also increases.)

**A mouthpiece depart from your mouth.**

⇒ Even if a mouthpiece depart, return it back to your mouth with a calm mind and you can push out water gathering in the hose by blowing out a breath swiftly.

---

I wore a swim fin and Scuba diving started finally. Once I dived, I became panicky immediately. Because both of mouth and nose were covered in the water, I felt anxiety rapidly. As time goes, I could grasp the techniques of breathing in water. And I had a relax attitude and began to enjoy scenery around myself. But at that time I felt ear pain suddenly due to change of atmospheric pressure. Although I plugged my ear and then ear pain subsided a bit temporally, I became panicky.

I gradually became used to in the sea and then I felt enjoying the beautiful view. I could enjoy seeing many kind of creatures.Small fishes living coral reef and Sea turtle swimming with Black shark and so on.  
Suddenly that appeared in front of me. A 5 meters Whale shark swam through me near in a relaxed manner.

Because I could not believe that I was able to see Whale sharks near, I was very excited. Including Scuba diving I had good experience so much.

## Osmena Peak
After we returned to the spot for whale shark, I started to look for the accommodation alone. Although other Japanese had plan to return to Cebu city and sightsee there, I wanted to climb Osmena Peak absolutely. Osmena Peak is the highest mountain in Cebu island. the height of this mountain is only 1015 meter. Moreover It takes only 20 minutes to the top of the mountain because the trail has been improved for visitor.

This mountain was easy to climb. But this activity was very attractive for me because I had wished to climb mountains for long time.

Although I did not know where the suitable accommodation was located, I started to look for an accommodation and relied to a local motorcycle taxi driver. I talked with the young driver that I would like to find a reasonable hotel and would like you to introduce it to me. And then he introduced the hotel to me at once. I asked the owner of the hotel how much accommodation fee is and he allowed me to decide the fee freely. I paid 800 peso to him.

Although There was a roof and a door which is able to lock in the room, there was no a window. I was bitten many times by mosquitoes during night. I was accompanied by severe itching. A mosquito has possibility to transmit many mosquito-borne disease. Thus I recommend a room having windows.

Next morning I took a motorcycle taxi to Daragete (700 peso). I transferred to more powerful motorcycle and went to the entrance for the mountain (round-trip transportation expenses : 300 peso).
